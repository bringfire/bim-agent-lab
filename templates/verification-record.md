# Verification record template

- **Change and revision:** feature, branch/commit, and date.
- **Environment:** OS, host application and point release, build/runtime versions.
- **Fixture:** source, prepared version, case IDs, scope, and reset state.
- **Independent expected answers:** how were these established?
- **Checks run:** exact command or repeatable application steps.
- **Observed results:** output, quantities/units, screenshots or reports where useful.
- **Comparison:** pass/fail for each acceptance case; tolerance and rounding basis.
- **Model effects:** evidence of whether queries changed the model.
- **Checks not run:** explicit missing environments or unresolved limitations.
- **Conclusion:** precisely what the evidence supports.
