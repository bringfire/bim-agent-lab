# Feature brief template

Copy this template and replace the prompts below.

- **BIM problem and user:** who needs what decision or action?
- **Intended use:** what will the result support?
- **Inputs and scope:** document, categories, phases, links, units, and requirement sources.
- **Expected result:** a concrete example, with traceable evidence.
- **Acceptance cases:** one valid case, one failure, one exception/exclusion, and independent expected answers.
- **Existing work:** relevant code, precedents, dependencies, and license considerations.
- **Proposed change:** affected components and the smallest complete implementation.
- **Verification:** checks possible offline and checks requiring the host application.
- **Limits and open questions:** what remains unverified or outside this change?
- **Teaching outcome:** what should another professional learn from the work?
