# Research record template

- **Question and date:** what uncertainty are we investigating?
- **Original source:** official URL or upstream repository.
- **Version:** release, full commit, documentation version, and local checkout path if used.
- **License evidence:** exact file/revision and component-specific terms.
- **Dependencies:** host/runtime, direct packages, transitive concerns, and native requirements.
- **Source observations:** relevant files and what they establish.
- **Local tests:** exact commands/application setup, outcomes, and evidence; write “not run” where applicable.
- **Potential role:** development tool, runtime dependency, adapted code, or precedent.
- **Decision:** adopt, adapt, investigate further, or reject; give the reason.
- **Outstanding checks:** compatibility, behavior, distribution, or completeness questions.
